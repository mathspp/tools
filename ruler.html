A full-screen browser ruler with millimetre ticks and centimetre labels along the top and left edges of the viewport.

The browser maps CSS centimetres to screen pixels, so this is best for layout and on-screen measurement. Physical accuracy depends on your browser, display scaling, zoom level, and operating system settings.
